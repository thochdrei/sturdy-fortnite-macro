PIRATE SHOP MACRO - START HERE
==============================

1. Install Python (one time)
   Download from https://www.python.org/downloads/
   IMPORTANT: tick "Add Python to PATH" on the first screen of the installer.
   (3.12 or 3.13 is best.)

2. Double-click  "Build EXE.bat"
   Wait a few minutes until it says SUCCESS.
   This makes "Pirate Shop Macro.exe" in the same folder.

3. Double-click  "Pirate Shop Macro.exe"
   Click Yes on the administrator prompt. The app opens on the SETUP page.

4. On the Setup page, press "Capture" next to each step.
   - Get that button on your screen in Fortnite
   - Press F9
   - Drag a box around the button
   When every step has a green check, you're done.

5. Go to the Dashboard and press START.
   You get a few seconds to click into Fortnite.

To stop: press F8 (works inside the game) or move your mouse to the top-left corner.


BUILD FAILED, OR THE EXE WON'T OPEN?
------------------------------------
Use the backup method instead:
  a) Double-click "Install Requirements.bat" (one time)
  b) Double-click "pirate_shop_macro.pyw"
It's the exact same app.

Still stuck? Read README.md, especially the Troubleshooting section.
